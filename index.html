How the estimate works
  1. Pool. Take the 2025 finishers, sort them by finish time, and keep a fixed percentile band (default 10th–75th). That drops the elites and the back of the field, whose pacing doesn't transfer well.
  2. Plan. Before any checkpoint is entered, the plan shows the typical splits of pool runners who finished close to the goal time.
  3. Projection. Once a time is entered at a checkpoint, each pool runner's later splits are scaled to it. A runner who reached Luxmore in 2:00 and finished in 7:30 becomes "×3.75 from Luxmore". Runners whose time at that checkpoint was closest to the entered one get the most weight.
  4. Range. The estimate is the weighted median. The range shown is the middle 50% (25th–75th percentile), and the chart shows the full spread.

How accurate is it? Tested on the 2025 field (each runner projected from the others), the estimate was unbiased. Its typical miss was about 16 min from Luxmore Hut, 12 min from Iris Burn and 6 min from Moturau. Half of runners finished inside the range shown.

It only uses the latest checkpoint entered. Weather, a bad patch or a great descent can move things a lot, especially early on.
